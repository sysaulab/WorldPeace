
# TECHNICAL ANNEX: THE COMMON SENSE PROTOCOL (CSP)

**Version:** 0.9 (Draft for Community Review)  
**Classification:** Open Source / Public Infrastructure  
**Core Philosophy:** *Minimum Regret over Absolute Truth*

---

## 1. OVERVIEW & MISSION

The Common Sense Protocol (CSP) is a decentralized, open-source intelligence (OSINT) clearinghouse designed for a post-nationalist world. 

- **Mission:** To reduce the amplitude of unavoidable human errors in global decision-making by providing a transparent, probabilistic, and source-verifiable record of geopolitical events and military dispositions.
- **Exclusion Policy:** None. Any human (or collective) may contribute, provided they accept the transparency requirements of the ledger.
- **Core Principle:** We do not tell you what to do. We tell you what we think, how confident we are, and what the cost of being wrong will be. You choose your own risk.

---

## 2. DATA LAYER: STORAGE & ADDRESSING

**2.1. Primary Backbone: InterPlanetary File System (IPFS)**

- All raw data (satellite imagery, intercepted radio transcripts, witness statements, radar telemetry) is stored on IPFS using Content Identifiers (CIDs).
- **Immutability:** Once a datum is pinned, its CID is permanent. No state or actor can retroactively alter or delete a verified upload without breaking the chain of custody.

**2.2. Persistent Storage: The Pinning Cooperative**

- **Problem:** IPFS is addressing, not hosting. Data dies if no one pins it.
- **Solution:** A voluntary DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) where members stake a minor amount of stablecoin or computational resources to guarantee the pinning of high-priority datasets.
- **Redundancy Rule:** Critical datasets (e.g., nuclear silo movements) must be pinned on at least 5 geographically distinct nodes across 3 different jurisdictions.

**2.3. The Public Ledger (The "Regret Chain")**

- A lightweight, append-only blockchain (or a secure, signed Merkle DAG) that stores only the *metadata* and *consensus conclusions*.
- *Does NOT store raw data* (to keep it lean), but stores the CIDs pointing to the raw data.
- **Immutability:** Once a consensus entry is committed, it cannot be erased—only amended by a subsequent entry (creating a chain of corrections).

## 3. CONSENSUS MECHANISM: THE TRIANGULATION RULE

**3.1. The "3 or More" Threshold**

- No single analyst's claim is recorded as a "Commonality" on the ledger.
- A conclusion is elevated to the public ledger **only** when 3 or more independent analysts (or analyst teams) submit analyses that converge on the same core inference (e.g., "Unit X is at Location Y").

**3.2. Source Diversity Mandate**

- A consensus is **invalid** if all 3 concurring analyses derive from the same source lineage (e.g., 3 analysts all using the same single Telegram channel).
- For a valid entry, the system automatically checks for source diversity:
    - **Optical** (satellite)
    - **SIGINT** (radio/radar intercepts)
    - **HUMINT** (witness accounts)
    - **Technical** (railway logs, fuel purchases)
- If diversity is insufficient, the entry is flagged as **"Narrow Consensus"** (higher risk of groupthink).

**3.3. Dissenting Minority Reports**

- A single analyst with a demonstrably high historical accuracy (see Section 4) can file an "Urgent Minority Report."
- This bypasses the 3-person rule for *early warning* purposes, but remains flagged as unconfirmed until triangulation is achieved.

## 4. REPUTATION & ERROR PROFILING

**4.1. No Gatekeeping—Only Transparency**

- We do not ban or silence anyone. Instead, every analyst accumulates a public **Error Profile** recorded on the ledger.

**4.2. The Error Profile (EP) Metrics**

- **Calibration Score:** *"When Analyst X says 80% confidence, they are actually correct 65% of the time."* (Bias: +15% over-optimism).
- **False Positive Rate:** How often they call a strike that never happens.
- **False Negative Rate:** How often they miss a strike that does happen.
- **Amplitude Bias:** Do they tend to overestimate or underestimate the severity of consequences?

**4.3. Weighting**

- The system does not *exclude* low-accuracy analysts. It merely applies a **reputation weight (0.0 - 1.0)** when calculating aggregate consensus.
- The user sees the *unweighted* consensus alongside the *weighted* consensus, so they can judge the difference for themselves.

---

## 5. THE AMPLITUDE METADATA SCHEMA

Every consensus entry on the ledger must include a **three-vector amplitude tag**, quantifying the impact of being wrong.

| Vector | Scale (0-10) | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Magnitude (M)** | 0-10 | The scale of physical, economic, or human damage if the prediction is wrong and acted upon. (10 = Nuclear war / Genocide; 1 = Minor diplomatic spat). |
| **Temporal Urgency (T)** | 0-10 | The decision-speed required. (10 = Act within minutes; 1 = Strategic trends over decades). |
| **Reversibility (R)** | 0-10 | Can the damage be undone? (10 = Completely reversible, say sorry and move on; 0 = Permanently catastrophic). |

**Example Entry:**
> *"Consensus: 78% probability of Oreshnik strike on European radar sites within 72 hours. M=9, T=10, R=2. Source: 2x Optical, 1x SIGINT. Weighted Confidence: 74%."*

## 6. USER INTERFACE: THE RISK FILTER

- The ledger is raw. The presentation is **customizable**.
- Users (generals, diplomats, journalists, aid workers, citizens) set their own **Risk Tolerance Sliders**.
    - *"Only show me entries where M > 7 AND T > 8."* (Crisis mode).
    - *"Only show me entries where M < 4 AND R > 6."* (Operational safety mode).
    - *"Show me everything, but highlight entries with high dissenting variance."* (Researcher mode).
- This ensures the network serves everyone, from the paranoid commander to the cautious humanitarian.

---

## 7. GOVERNANCE & SECURITY

**7.1. Governance Model: The Cooperative**

- No central leadership. Key protocol decisions (e.g., changing the triangulation rule, adjusting weighting algorithms) are put to a vote weighted by **long-term participation and accuracy**, not wealth or identity.

**7.2. Sybil Defense**

- New analysts are given an initial weight of 0.1 until they have made at least 10 verifiable predictions.
- You cannot fake a track record; history is on the ledger.

**7.3. The "Burn the Network" Threat**

- If a state actor attempts to physically or digitally dismantle the nodes, the IPFS data persists. The ledger is mirrored on USB drives, printed on paper archives, and buried in community libraries.
- The network is not a server. It is a **living document**. You cannot kill it without killing the people who carry it.

---

## 8. ETHICAL BOUNDARY

- We are a **clearinghouse**, not a military command.
- We **never** recommend action. We provide probabilities, source lineages, and amplitude vectors. The decision to act—and the responsibility for that act—remains entirely with the user.
- By doing so, we reject the danger of "absolute truth" and embrace the humility of "minimum regret."
