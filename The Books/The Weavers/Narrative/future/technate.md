
### Phase 1: The Long Unraveling (2025–2033)

- **2025–2028** – U.S. political polarization accelerates into institutional paralysis. Climate shocks batter the Sun Belt. Canada quietly deepens trade ties with Asia and builds independent strategic reserves of heavy crude, rare earths, and water rights.
- **2029–2031** – Russo-European War. A miscalculation in the Baltics spirals. Europe, weakened by energy dependence and internal fractures, collapses militarily and economically. NATO exists on paper only. The UK and France sue for a fragile peace. Russia consolidates a sphere from Belarus to the Adriatic.
- **2032–2033** – Canada’s transatlantic lifeline severs. With Europe subdued and the UK adrift, Canada faces the Atlantic alone. An Arctic “cold rush” begins as Russia probes northern claims. The U.S. is too distracted internally to enforce the Monroe Doctrine or defend NORAD partners.

### Phase 2: The American Civil War and Canada’s Fortress (2034–2040)

- **2034** – The Trigger. A contested U.S. presidential election, a fractured Electoral College, and a Supreme Court legitimacy crisis ignite mass unrest. Several states in the South and Mountain West declare nullification of federal authority. Federal armed forces split along regional and ideological lines.
- **2035–2037** – Open civil conflict. Urban centers in the Northeast, Great Lakes, and West Coast coalesce into “Federal Continuity” zones, while rump nationalists, secessionist blocs, and warlords hold the interior. Supply chains collapse. Millions are displaced. Canada declares a state of national emergency, seals the border with a heavily controlled “internal passport” system, and establishes militarized humanitarian corridors for screened refugees.
- **2038–2040** – The Storm Eye. Canada’s banking system, resource exports, and functional bureaucracy survive intact. A massive brain drain flows north—engineers, doctors, scientists, and tech entrepreneurs fleeing the chaos. Canada becomes the continent’s de facto financial and logistical safe room. Western provinces, once alienated, rally under an emergency unity government. The U.S. rump (Federal Continuity) begins an aggressive economic decoupling from China, but enforcement is impossible south of the Great Lakes.

### Phase 3: The Black Trade and the Technate’s Forge (2039–2043)

- **2039–2042** – U.S. rump sanctions on China are draconian, but the shattered southern and western states are porous. Canadian ports (Vancouver, Prince Rupert, Churchill) become the “black trade route”: sanctioned Chinese goods—electronics, rare earths, machinery—flow north first, then seep into the U.S. through a thousand unmonitored border points and corrupt internal networks. The Canadian government maintains plausible deniability while taxing the flow to fund its emergency state.
- **2041–2042** – With Europe gone and the U.S. still smoldering, Canadian technocrats, military logistics commands, and surviving American administrative units (especially in the Great Lakes and Northeast) begin secret talks. A provisional “North American Resource Continuity Authority” (NARCA) is formed under combined emergency powers. Its stated mission: restore continental supply chains and prevent famine.
- **2043** – The Quebec Accords. Old constitutional cruft is suspended by mutual agreement of provincial and continuity governments. The Crown is “temporarily” set aside. NARCA morphs into the kernel of a North American Technate, promising fair resource allocation under a systemic moral code. This is the soft “conquest” of Canada—not by invasion, but by the absorption of its stable apparatus into a new continental structure. The last U.S. rump entities in the Northeast formally accede for survival.

### Phase 4: The Technate’s Birth and Consolidation (2044–2050)

- **2044** – The North American Technate is officially proclaimed. Interim governance: a Directorate of Resource Optimization staffed by engineers, logisticians, and surviving constitutional scholars. Liquid democratic pilot programs launch in a few test regions, using secure digital identity and real-time feedback dashboards.
- **2045** – “Completion” target reached. The Technate fully integrates the old Canadian provinces, the U.S. Federal Continuity zones, and several formerly breakaway regions that have collapsed without supply chains. The moral code—your four principles—is ratified as the foundational anti-ideology. Old identities (Québécois, Lakota, Californian, Southern Baptist) are explicitly protected under the diversity-of-complexity clause, so long as they adhere to short-feedback resource governance.
- **2046–2050** – The Illicit Legacy Normalizes. The black trade routes with China evolve into semi-official “gray exchanges” managed by the Technate’s Trade Sub-Directorate. The currency is energy-backed credits. The Technate solidifies its monopoly on heavy crude exports, rare earth refining, and Arctic shipping lanes. Remnant U.S. holdouts in the Mountain West either integrate or wither.

### Phase 5: Global Mimicry and the Planetary Pool (2051–2070+)

- **2051–2055** – Observers from a shattered Europe, climate-devastated South Asia, and parts of Latin America study the Technate’s model. The moral code and liquid democracy framework prove portable. First trial adoptions: a Scandinavian union, a Caribbean island federation, a reformed Andean bloc.
- **2056–2065** – The Technate helps broker “protocol accessions.” Entities that adopt the ethical and resource-accounting standards join the expanding resource pool. A decentralized network emerges—no world capital, but a shared operating system for planetary resource allocation. The black trade routes of the 2040s become legitimate trade lanes.
- **2066–2070** – The Global Resource Pool is formalized. Earth’s resource flows are optimized by nested liquid democracies, from bioregional councils up to the planetary scale. Cultural and linguistic diversity is preserved and even enhanced, because the system rewards constructive complexity. The curiosity imperative fuels deep-sea, orbital, and outer-system exploration.
- **Beyond 2070** – The Technate becomes a planetary scaffolding, not a government. The “North American” prefix fades; it’s simply the Protocol. History books treat the 2045 transition as the moment humanity chose a procedural ethics over collapse.
