## Invitation to a Story Project

### What is this project?

We are creating a novel set in a future where humanity has encountered intelligent non-human life. The story will be built from the real, lived experiences of people like you—especially those whose voices are rarely centred.

This isn’t only a book. It’s a community economic experiment. Half of all revenue is split equally among the contributors who choose to share their personal stories. The other half goes into a community development fund, governed democratically by those same contributors. Your life, honestly told, becomes the soil for a new kind of fiction—and you share directly in what it earns.

---

### How does it work?

**Step 1: A conversation with a peer worker**  
You’ll sit down with a peer worker—someone who shares some of your life experience, who knows the margins, and who is trained in deep, respectful listening. This is not an interrogation. It’s a gentle, unhurried conversation where you tell your life story in your own words, at your own pace. The peer worker’s job is to listen, to hold space, and to make sure you feel safe and heard.

**Meanwhile, a prompter observes quietly.**  
In the same room, a skilled narrative guide sits to the side, taking notes on a computer. They are not the interviewer. They are listening for the shape of your story—the themes, the turning points, the moments that hold the deepest meaning. Later, they will gently organize what you’ve shared into a flowing narrative, always staying true to your words.

**An AI assists, but it’s not the star.**  
After the session, the prompter uses a writing tool (an AI) to help assemble your story into a **personal account booklet**. The AI is simply a digital scribe—it helps arrange your own words into a clean, readable story, following the prompter’s guidance. The warmth came from the peer worker. The craft comes from the prompter. The booklet is yours.

**Step 2: Your choice — the real exchange**  
Once your booklet is complete and you’ve had a chance to review it, you face a single, meaningful decision:

- **Share it.** You give us permission to use your booklet as source material for the novel. Your story will inspire characters, themes, and moments in a book that regenerates itself for each reader in their chosen tone and setting. You won’t control those fictional transformations, but you will always own your original booklet. In return, you become a full economic partner: an equal share of 50% of all project revenue, and an equal vote in how the community development fund is spent.
- **Keep it private.** You walk away with your booklet and the experience of having been deeply heard. You receive no money from the project. You owe nothing. The witnessing itself is the gift.

This is the honest exchange at the heart of our project. Sharing asks something real of you. We honour that with real partnership.

---

### Your rights as a sharing contributor

- **Equal pay.** Every contributor who shares their booklet receives an identical, equal share of 50% of all revenue. Presence is the only measure.
- **Democratic voice.** You help decide, collectively with other contributors, how the other 50% — the community development fund — is used.
- **Booklet ownership.** Your personal booklet remains yours, always. You can publish it, pass it to your children, or keep it private. Sharing it with the project does not transfer ownership.
- **Withdrawal window.** You may change your mind and withdraw your permission up until the novel’s first public release.

---

### What does “money with strings attached” mean?

The string is the act of sharing itself. By allowing your real life to flow into a generative fictional world, you take a small risk. You let go of control over how your story’s echoes appear in the novel. The money is not a wage — it’s a share of the harvest from a field you helped plant. Accepting it means accepting that you have given something precious to strangers. We believe that kind of giving deserves a return, and also that it should be named honestly.

---

### Why participate, even if you don’t share?

Many people have found that being truly heard—with patience, without an agenda—is a rare and quietly powerful experience. The peer worker will offer you that. Your personal booklet may become a treasured document for you and your family, whether or not it ever enters a novel. There is no charge, no hidden agenda. You can stop at any time.

---

### Why participate, if you do share?

- Your life helps build a vision of the future that is grounded in real human complexity, not stereotypes.
- You join a community where resources are shared fairly and decisions are made together.
- You help prove that art can be made ethically, with dignity and genuine partnership at its centre.

---

### Questions?

We are happy to talk through anything. There is no commitment until after you’ve completed your booklet and fully understand the choice before you. The first step is simply a conversation with a peer worker.

**Welcome. Your story is valuable. The decision of what to do with it is yours.**
