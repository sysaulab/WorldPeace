
**Welcome to the Circle**

Welcome to the circle. We live in a traditional way on the little land we have left. First, introduce yourself—your name, your people, what brings you here. Share what you can: food, a blanket, a story, your hands. No fire; we're in the city and we honour that law. And when a law doesn't serve the people, we petition to change it, we don't break it. No illegal drugs, no violence, no theft. Words heal or harm, so speak in a good way. Feed Elders and children first. Clean up after yourself, always—this land is a relative. Quiet from sundown to sunrise, unless ceremony calls. No photos without permission. If you bring a guest or a dog, you're responsible. And when you leave, say goodbye and take everything with you. You're in the circle now—let it lift you up.
