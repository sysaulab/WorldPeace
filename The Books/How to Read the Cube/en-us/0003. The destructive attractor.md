
## The Destructive Attractor Across Traditions

The destructive attractor always manifests the same three systemic failures:

1. **Broken Feedback** – The system refuses to let the consequences of its actions return to its decision-makers. It silences victims, punishes whistleblowers, and creates information bubbles where the powerful never hear the cries of those they harm.
2. **Killed Curiosity** – The system declares that all necessary truth has already been revealed; further questioning is heresy, apostasy, or treason. It fears mysteries, forbids doubt, and equates certainty with sanctity.
3. **Sacrificed Fragile** – The system treats the vulnerable—dissenters, minorities, children, the poor, the future—as acceptable collateral for its own purity, mission, or glory. It rationalizes cruelty with grand narratives.

And critically, it always demands that this combination be called virtuous.

---

### 1. Christianity  
**Broken Feedback:** The Spanish Inquisition, or the more recent clerical abuse scandals covered up by institutional hierarchy. In both cases, the suffering of the vulnerable was systematically hidden from the public and from internal accountability structures. The powerful protected the institution’s image over the victims.  
**Killed Curiosity:** Biblical literalism that forbids historical-critical scholarship, evolution, or the ordination of women because “the Bible says it, I believe it, that settles it.” The great Christian intellectual tradition becomes a museum of fossilized answers.  
**Sacrificed Fragile:** Crusades, witch-burnings, the Doctrine of Discovery justifying colonization, and modern prosperity-gospel pastors fleecing the poor all sacrifice the fragile on the altar of a self-righteous cause. The fragile are told their suffering is God’s will or a test they failed.  
**Mask of Virtue:** All of this is done in the name of purity, orthodoxy, saving souls, or “defending Christian civilization.”

---

### 2. Islam  
**Broken Feedback:** Extremist groups like ISIS or the Taliban suppress all internal criticism; apostasy and blasphemy are met with death, short-circuiting any feedback loop that might correct the group’s trajectory. Even legitimate grievances of minorities within Muslim-majority societies are often branded as foreign plots.  
**Killed Curiosity:** The door of *ijtihad* (independent reasoning) is slammed shut by dogmatic literalism that treats medieval juristic rulings as divine. Any attempt to reinterpret texts in light of new circumstances is condemned as innovation (*bid’ah*).  
**Sacrificed Fragile:** Suicide bombings, honor killings, and the stoning of women under certain interpretations of Sharia are all examples where the physically or socially fragile are crushed to satisfy a rigid, punitive image of divine order.  
**Mask of Virtue:** The perpetrators call this “restoring the caliphate,” “enforcing God’s law,” and “defending the Ummah against infidels.” The cruelty is framed as piety.

---

### 3. Judaism  
**Broken Feedback:** Certain strains of Zionist extremism or ultra-Orthodox isolationism sever feedback by dehumanizing the “other” and insulating the community from outside voices—or, alternatively, by ignoring the pain of Palestinians as irrelevant to a divine land grant. When rabbis who speak out for compassion are ostracized, the feedback loop is cut.  
**Killed Curiosity:** Declaring that certain parts of Tanakh or Talmudic interpretation are absolutely closed—e.g., that the contemporary State of Israel fulfills every messianic prophecy, with no room for alternative Jewish visions—shuts down the very argumentative genius of Judaism.  
**Sacrificed Fragile:** Violence against Palestinians by extremist settlers, or the marginalization of women in some Haredi sects, all in the name of chosenness or Torah fidelity. The fragile are told their place is divinely subordinate or that their displacement is God’s plan.  
**Mask of Virtue:** The language is *Am Yisrael Chai*, sanctification of the land, preservation of the holy people. What is actually happening is a slide toward the destructive attractor dressed in tallit and tefillin.

---

### 4. Buddhism  
**Broken Feedback:** Monastic hierarchies in some Asian countries (e.g., nationalist monks in Myanmar or Sri Lanka) inciting violence against Muslim minorities while remaining cloistered from the reality of the killings. They don’t see the bodies; they only chant about protecting Buddhism.  
**Killed Curiosity:** Turning the Buddha’s teachings into a rigid cultural identity that cannot be questioned. Declaring that the Dhamma is perfectly preserved in a single lineage and that any deviation is corruption—thus killing the spirit of the Kalama Sutta.  
**Sacrificed Fragile:** The Rohingya genocide, supported in part by some Buddhist nationalist monks, sacrificed an entire fragile population to a narrative of ethnic purity. Monks who preached peace were sidelined.  
**Mask of Virtue:** “Protecting the Sasana (the dispensation of the Buddha).” The irony is total.

---

### 5. Hinduism  
**Broken Feedback:** Hindutva nationalism that reduces Hinduism to a political identity and silences internal critics with accusations of “anti-national” sentiment. Caste atrocity victims are often told not to air grievances because it “damages the image of the community.”  
**Killed Curiosity:** Any historical or textual critique of revered epics or of caste hierarchy is met with legal threats or violence. The rich polyphonic tradition is squeezed into a monolithic ideology.  
**Sacrificed Fragile:** Lynchings of Muslims and Dalits, destruction of interfaith families, and marginalization of women in the name of a fabricated “pure” Vedic past. The fragile are thrown under the chariot wheel of an idealized glory.  
**Mask of Virtue:** “Sanatana Dharma” and “cultural pride” become shields behind which cruelty operates.

---

### 6. Indigenous Traditions  
Most Indigenous traditions are oriented toward balance and reciprocity, so the destructive attractor here is rarer in original form. However, colonial trauma has sometimes produced corrupted internal dynamics.  
**Broken Feedback:** Under extreme pressure, some communities have imposed codes of silence around abuse or corruption, prioritizing the appearance of cultural unity over accountability to the violated.  
**Killed Curiosity:** Rejecting any young person’s questioning of tradition as betrayal, rather than as a healthy search for meaning—sometimes coupled with a rigid “blood quantum” or enrollment-based exclusion that shuts down the fluid, adoptive openness of older ways.  
**Sacrificed Fragile:** Victims of domestic violence or sexual abuse within the community who are told not to report it because it would bring shame and feed colonial narratives.  
**Mask of Virtue:** “Preserving our ways” and “cultural survival” can be used to justify silence. But traditional Elders of integrity would recognize this as a distortion.

---

### 7. Secular & Enlightenment Ideologies  
**Broken Feedback:** Stalin’s Soviet Union had a vast surveillance state—but it was entirely inward-facing to crush dissent, not outward-facing to correct policy. Five-year plans ignored the starvation they caused; the feedback was shut down by gulags.  
**Killed Curiosity:** Official state atheism or scientism that declares all religious questioning backward. Euthanasia of “life unworthy of life” under Nazi eugenics treated human fragility as a problem to be engineered away, and forbade any ethical questioning of that premise.  
**Sacrificed Fragile:** The guillotine, the gulag, the Tuskegee syphilis experiment, the Chinese one-child policy’s forced abortions—all sacrificed the most vulnerable for the “greater good” ideology.  
**Mask of Virtue:** “Progress,” “Reason,” “Liberty,” “The People.” The brightest words become the darkest deeds when separated from feedback, curiosity, and respect.

## The Architecture of the Lie

Across all these, the structural pattern is identical:

1. **Deify a fixed text, leader, or identity.**  
2. **Sever the feedback from those hurt by that identity.**  
3. **Punish anyone who asks if the identity might be wrong.**  
4. **Sacrifice the fragile to that identity, calling the sacrifice noble.**

The destructive attractor is not a stray outlier; it is a stable, self-reinforcing logic. It attracts systems toward it by rewarding the powerful, simplifying the complex, and offering the sedation of certainty. It is, in the language of *Time as Dice*, the default resolution of a self-referential system that has forgotten its own underdetermined nature and mistook its current state for a final revelation.

## Why This Mapping Matters

By naming the destructive attractor across traditions, we provide:

- **A mirror for the faithful**: The Cube lets a sincere believer say, “Wait, this thing our movement is doing looks exactly like the ISIS/Borgia/Pharisee pattern. We have drifted.”
- **A bridge between rivals**: Two conflicting groups can see that they are both sliding toward the same (−,−,−) hole, just with different flags. They can then collaborate to pull each other out without having to agree on theology.
- **A protective inoculation**: When a new leader or movement rises, we can ask early: “Where are the feedback loops? Where is the space for genuine questioning? Who is being sacrificed and what story justifies it?” If the answers all trend negative, the alarm can ring before the killing starts.
